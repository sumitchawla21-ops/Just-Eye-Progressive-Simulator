# JUST EYE Progressive Vision Simulator — Prototype

Purpose:
A showroom education app that uses the phone's live camera and simulates three progressive-lens designs with progressively wider usable fields of view.

## Modes
1. Conventional — narrow simulated usable field
2. Improved — wider simulated usable field
3. Advanced — widest simulated usable field

Planned comparisons:
- 1 vs 2
- 1 vs 3
- 2 vs 3
- 1 vs 2 vs 3

## Important
This is a visual sales/education prototype, not an optical measurement tool. The FOV shapes are illustrative and should not be presented as exact performance of any specific lens.

## Build
Open this folder in Android Studio Quail 4 (2026.1.4) or later.
Use JDK 17.
The project targets SDK 36 and uses CameraX 1.6.2 and Compose BOM 2026.09.00.
